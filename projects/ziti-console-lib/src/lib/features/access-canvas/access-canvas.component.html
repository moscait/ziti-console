Using the canvas
  • Zoom: mouse wheel, pinch, ⌘/Ctrl+scroll, or + and −. 0 fits everything.
  • Pan: drag empty space, or scroll with two fingers. Shift+drag selects several.
  • Group membership: drag a card into another group to move it (hold Alt/Option to copy), onto empty space to take it out, or a loose card onto a group to add it.
  • Access: drag an identity (or a whole identity group) onto a service.
  • Copy access: ⌘/Ctrl+C a card, select another card or a group, ⌘/Ctrl+V. Paste on empty space makes a copy.
  • More: right-click anything. Double-click a card to check its access.
